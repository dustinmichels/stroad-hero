# Changelog

## Oct 20, 2026

### Added

- **Frontage road & multi-business commercial depth**: Added a perpendicular right-angle connector road off the eastbound stroad east of Cramped Inn leading across the grass swale median into a two-lane frontage road with curved corner fillets and a 6-segment radiused centerline transition. Frontage road runs parallel to the highway with double yellow striping, white edge lines, driveway aprons, streetlights, and landscaping. Added three commercial business sub-scenes: Consumer Car Care auto repair & express wash modeled after `samples/buildings/frontage-road/car-repair.png` (red mansard roof, two-story white stucco volume, service garage bays, drive-through wash canopy, tire rack, roadside pylon sign, curbside mailbox), Leaders Outdoor furniture showroom, and a Checkers-style walk-up restaurant (`buildings/consumer_car_care.tscn`, `buildings/frontage_retail.tscn`, `buildings/frontage_commercial.tscn`, `main.tscn`).

- **Southern live oak tree**: Modeled the commercial stroad live oak from `samples/props/trees/tree1.png` in Blender with a stout flared trunk, visible buttress roots, low spreading limbs, a distinct leaning secondary trunk, and an irregular rounded canopy assembled from smaller foliage clusters over a reddish-brown mulch bed (`props/tree1.blend`). Added it as `StreetTree` variant `TreeType.LIVE_OAK`, configured cylinder collision and interactive foliage rustle, and populated live oaks along the frontage road median and strip mall landscaping (`props/tree.tscn`, `props/tree.gd`, `main.tscn`).

- **Florida palm tree**: Modeled the reference sabal palm in Blender with a tapered ringed trunk, layered fan fronds, and a hanging skirt of dead leaves (`props/tree2.blend`). Added it as a collidable `StreetTree` variant and placed palms in parking-lot and frontage landscaping (`props/tree.tscn`, `props/tree.gd`, `main.tscn`).
- **Ford F-150 truck model**: Created a high-fidelity 3D model of the 14th-generation Ford F-150 SuperCrew pickup truck in Blender (`cars/bodies/f150.blend`), imported into Godot with full material mapping (`cars/bodies/f150.tscn`, `cars/f150.tscn`). Features iconic twin horizontal chrome grille bars with center blue oval emblem, signature C-clamp LED daytime running lights with stacked projector headlights, stepped front door beltline dip, SuperCrew 4-door cab, sculpted power dome hood, F-150 fender badges, side mirrors with amber signals, cab-length running boards, short bed with black protective rail caps and corrugated bed liner, C-bracket taillights, heavy chrome bumpers, and 6-spoke alloy truck wheels (`cars/car.gd`, `cars/passenger_car.gd`, `tests/main_tests.gd`).
- **Day & weather simulation system**: Replaced the static 3-way toggle with an accelerated game day running continuously from 3:30 PM to 9:00 PM (5.5 game hours per day, defaulting to 1 real second per game minute). As time progresses, the sky, sun angle, sun energy, and ambient lighting smoothly darken from afternoon daylight to golden sunset to twilight to night, looping back to 3:30 PM with day tracking (`game_state.gd`, `main.gd`).
- **Weather and compass HUD widget**: Added a unified top-right HUD display with 12-hour formatted time (e.g. 3:30 PM), time period indicator (AFTERNOON, GOLDEN HOUR, SUNSET, TWILIGHT, NIGHT), wind speed and 16-point cardinal readout (e.g. "WIND: 12 MPH ENE"), and a circular compass dial with cardinal markers (N, E, S, W) and dynamic wind direction arrow aligned with camera yaw and in-world flags (`ui/weather_display.gd`, `ui/hud.gd`). Added `--test-day-weather` test in `tests/main_tests.gd`.
- **Dorf parking lot vehicle rule**: Disallowed Jeeps, sports cars, and taxis from the Dorf dealership parking lot (`ParkingLot.DISALLOWED_DORF_LOT_CAR_TYPES`, `ParkingLot.is_car_type_allowed_in_dorf_lot()`, `TrafficManager.is_car_allowed_in_dorf_lot()`). Updated seeded Dorf lot configurations to Ford-appropriate models (SUVs, pickups, minivans) and added verification assertions in `--test-verify-render`.
- **Offset jewelry billboard & billboard variants**: Added support for dual billboard styles within `props/billboard.tscn` via a `variant` property (`LAWYER_CENTERED` and `JEWELRY_OFFSET`), introduced a dedicated second scene `props/billboard_offset.tscn`, and placed it in the world along the frontage road median (`main.tscn`). The new variant reproduces the weathered Florida highway billboard from `samples/props/billboard/` featuring a corten rusted steel monopole offset to the right, asymmetric kicker struts, right-side access ladder leading to an extended maintenance cage platform, rooftop angled solar panels with photovoltaic cells, a bottom catwalk with central green Lamar nameplate, shifted collision geometry, and an authentic "Simon G. / Nature Coast Jewelers" advertisement with diamond engagement ring artwork (`props/billboard.gd`, `props/billboard.tscn`, `props/billboard_offset.tscn`, `props/billboard_jewelry_face.png`, `props/jewelry_ring.png`).
- **Neglected sidewalk weathering**: Replaced pristine concrete sidewalk visuals with authentic neglected suburban infrastructure detailing: procedural diagonal corner fractures and jagged transverse cracks, crabgrass and dandelion weed clusters sprouting through expansion joints and fissures, chipped curb edges with aggregate exposure, uneven slab settling normal step lips (trip hazards), aged slab tone shifts with mismatched municipal replacement slabs, gutter silt pooling, spalled concrete patches, and flattened chewing gum spots (`shaders/sidewalk.gdshader`, `main.tscn`).
- **Gritty vehicle paint textures**: Added procedural road-grit texturing to vehicles using triplanar roughness noise and micro-bump normal mapping (`cars/passenger_car.gd`). Applied automatically to utility and rugged vehicles (Pickup, F-150, Jeep) and a subset of commuting traffic while keeping sports cars polished and dealership lot cars showroom-clean (`traffic/traffic_manager.gd`, `tests/main_tests.gd`).

### Changed

- **Fast inner test suite**: Trimmed default `mise run test` down to 3 instant unit tests (`--test-find-blocker`, `--test-priority-arbitration`, `--test-exit-priority`) taking ~2.5s total. Relocated long physics simulations (`--test-merge-gap`, `--test-right-turn-following`, `--test-mats-blocker`) and secondary system tests to `mise run test --extended` (`scripts/run_tests.py`, `AGENTS.md`).
- **Subtle & varied exhaust smoke**: Softened car exhaust smoke particle opacity (peak alpha reduced from 0.65 to 0.28 with smooth dissipation) and replaced rigid uniform discs with procedural multi-lobed organic cloud puffs. Added particle-level variety (randomized billboard orientation, gentle angular spin, per-particle scale range 0.55-1.15, lifetime randomness, and tangential drift) as well as vehicle-class and per-car variation across particle amounts, lifetimes, and tint tones (`cars/car.gd`).
- **Map prop & tree layout clean-up**: Audited and adjusted roadside props in `main.tscn`. Shifted `Tree_72_11` out of the connector road into the median landscaping (`x=77, z=16.5`), moved `Streetlight_66_9.8` to `x=76, z=12.1` and `Flag_CrampedInnEast` to `x=61, z=15.85` clear of connector asphalt, and moved sidewalk-adjacent trees on both sides of the stroad back into grass buffers to eliminate trunk collisions with sidewalk curbs and walking paths.
## Oct 19, 2026

### Changed

- **Sedan appearance**: Lowered the cabin profile, reshaped the front and rear lamps, and added five-spoke wheel detail, grille blades, door seams, and beltline trim in `cars/bodies/sedan.blend`. Mirror housings now follow the body paint palette. Driving behavior and wheel radius are unchanged.
- **Daytime car headlights**: Car headlights now remain off during the day across all vehicle types (SpotLight3D disabled, mesh emission disabled). Headlights dynamically activate during evening and night for moving vehicles, turn off when parked, and update on time-of-day transitions (`cars/car.gd`, `cars/passenger_car.gd`, car scenes, `addons/mesh_baker/mesh_baker.gd`).
- **Lean test workflow**: Moved the Godot test harness out of `main.gd` into an inherited test scene, made `mise run test` a small inner suite, added `--extended` for long gameplay simulations, and moved non-asserting performance measurements to `mise run bench`. Removed duplicate merge and animation-implementation tests, trimmed brittle visual assertions, and made the runner stop failed Godot processes immediately.

### Added

- **Expanded parody objective system**: Added JSON-driven objective catalog (`objectives/mats_objectives.json`) and procedural dialogue generator for Mom's errand calls across 4 parody stores (Walgroins, Booger King, Taco Hell, Smell) and 13 items. Features Dutch terms of endearment, variable excuses, and randomized requests alternating between proper and generic store names.

- **Mats objectives (errands from Mom)**: An errand loop where Mom calls Mats with errands to pick up items along the stroad. Answer incoming calls via the `answer_phone` action (A, Enter, or Gamepad A) with a typewriter dialogue overlay that pauses Mats's input while the world keeps running. Errands alternate across the stroad (cough syrup at Walgroins, milk at Smell Food Mart, hamburgers at Booger King) to encourage crossing traffic. Includes an in-world emissive green chevron marker with an arrival ring (`objectives/objective_marker.gd`), an off-screen HUD tracker arrow with distance readouts (`ui/objective_arrow.gd`), a completion toast with checkmark (`ui/objective_toast.gd`), a green-bordered OBJ counter on the HUD (`ui/hud.tscn`, `ui/hud.gd`), and a headless test suite (`--test-objectives`).

### Fixed

- **Lot traffic collisions**: Cars backing out of stalls and heading back to the road no longer drive through each other. `TrafficManager.find_blocker` now checks each car's real footprint along its curved path, not a straight line from its center. A car backing out claims its whole back-out route, and `can_reverse_out` waits until that route is clear. When two routes cross, the car that reaches the crossing first goes and the other stops before it is in the way. The Taco Hell crossing mutex and the drive-thru/driveway special cases are gone because the general check covers them (`traffic/traffic_manager.gd`).
- **Parking-lot turn clearance**: Right-sized the three driveway openings and their asphalt aprons around the full entering and exiting car footprints so cars stay off the sidewalk without oversized curb cuts (`main.tscn`).
