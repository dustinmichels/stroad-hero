# Business Parodies

There are businesses in the game which are parodies of real businesses. For agentic clarity, here is a mapping.

- Burger King -> Booger King
- Taco Bell -> Taco Hell
- Walgreens -> Walgroins
- Ford -> Dorf
- Domino's Pizza -> abominos
- Strip Mall Businesses: USA Vape, Freedom Tattoo, abominos, Star Nails & Spa
