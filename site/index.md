## Mom has a few errands

Your phone rings. Answer it and Mom asks for something from a store along the stroad. An arrow points the way. Reach the store and the errand is done, until she remembers something else.

The day runs from 3:30 to 8:00 PM, three real minutes. The HUD counts objectives finished, crossings made, and SPD: Senseless Pedestrian Deaths.

## Along the stroad
