# The neighborhood

The game takes place along one stretch of stroad: two lanes each way, split by a double yellow line, with parking lots on both sides and concrete tunnel portals at each end. A chainlink fence and a forest close off the edges.

Every business is a parody of a real chain.

## North side

Across the street from where Mats starts.

| Business  | Parody of | Notes                                                                                                                    |
| --------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Walgroins | Walgreens | 24-hour pharmacy with a shared parking lot. Errand stop.                                                                 |
| Taco Hell | Taco Bell | Drive-thru, shares the Walgroins lot. Errand stop.                                                                       |
| Smell     | Shell     | Gas station with six pumps and a convenience store. Errand stop.                                                         |
| Dorf      | Ford      | Dealership with display cars and three Quick Pain service bays, a two-way service drive, and a very large American flag. |

## South side

Mats's side of the street.

| Business       | Parody of    | Notes                                                                |
| -------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Booger King    | Burger King  | Drive-thru with its own driveway. Errand stop.                       |
| Colonial Plaza | A strip mall | USA Vape, Freedom Tattoo, Abominos (Domino's), and Star Nails & Spa. |
| Cramped Inn    | Comfort Inn  | Hotel with its own lot.                                              |

Behind the south side, a two-lane frontage road serves Consumer Car Care, Leaders Outdoor, and Checkers.

## Street furniture

The sidewalks are narrow and weathered. Along the way are utility poles, fire hydrants, yellow bollards, dumpsters behind Booger King, four bus stops, flagpoles, and streetlights that come on at dusk.
