# Errands and scoring

## How an errand works

1. **The phone rings.** Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to answer.
2. **Mom asks for something.** Every call is put together from an opening, an excuse, and a request, so they rarely repeat: _"Mats schat [darling]... The smoke detector started doing that awful little beep! Can you stop by Walgroins and get some batteries?"_
3. **Follow the arrow.** The objective appears on the HUD, and an arrow and marker point to the store.
4. **Reach the store.** The errand is complete when Mats gets there. A toast confirms it, and the next call comes later.

## Where Mom sends you

| Store       | What it is       | What Mom asks for                                           |
| ----------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Walgroins   | 24-hour pharmacy | Cough syrup, band-aids, potato chips, toothpaste, batteries |
| Taco Hell   | Fast food        | Tacos, burritos, quesadillas                                |
| Booger King | Fast food        | Hamburgers                                                  |
| Smell       | Gas station      | Milk, cereal, chewing gum, beef jerky                       |

Booger King is on the south side of the stroad, the side where Mats starts. The other three are across the street.

## The HUD

The box in the top-left corner keeps three counts for the day:

| Counter    | What it counts                                  |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| SPD        | Senseless Pedestrian Deaths: times Mats got hit |
| Objectives | Errands finished                                |
| Crossings  | Complete crossings of the main stroad           |

When a car hits Mats, SPD goes up and he respawns on the sidewalk he started from.

The top-right corner shows the day number and the weather.

## The day

Each day runs from 3:30 to 8:00 PM and lasts three real minutes, so one real second is about 1.5 game minutes.

| Time    | What happens                        |
| ------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 3:30 PM | The day starts in full daylight     |
| 6:30 PM | Golden hour                         |
| 7:00 PM | Sunset begins                       |
| 7:15 PM | Streetlights and headlights turn on |
| 8:00 PM | The day ends                        |

At 8:00 PM the game pauses on a **Day over!** summary with the day's SPD, Objectives, and Crossings. **Start Day _N_** begins the next day: Mats and the shopping carts return to their starting spots, the road is cleaned up, and traffic starts over.
