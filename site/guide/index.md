# Getting started

Stroad Hero is a short, satirical game about getting around on foot in a town built for cars. You play Mats, a Dutch kid in Anywhere, USA. Mom calls with errands, the stores are across a four-lane stroad, and nobody built a crosswalk.

## Play

Download [Stroad Hero on itch.io](https://dustinmichels.itch.io/stroad-hero) for macOS, Windows, or Linux. A keyboard is required.

## Your first day

1. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the title screen to start.
2. When the phone rings, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to answer it. Mom tells you what she needs and where to get it.
3. Follow the arrow to the store. Walk with the arrow keys; they move Mats relative to the camera.
4. Wait for a gap, then cross. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to sprint for a few seconds.
5. Reach the store to finish the errand. Mom will call again.

The day ends at 8:00 PM, three real minutes after it starts. The summary shows how you did, and **Start Day 2** puts you back on the sidewalk with fresh traffic.

::: tip
Press <kbd>H</kbd> at any time to see the controls, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to pause.
:::
