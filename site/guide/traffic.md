# Traffic

Traffic in Stroad Hero isn't a pattern to memorize. Each car is simulated: it has a lane, a speed, and often an errand of its own.

## The fleet

Sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs, minivans, pickup trucks, a Jeep, sports cars, taxis, delivery vans, buses, semis, police SUVs, and a Cybertruck. Each class cruises at its own speed, and faster cars pass slower ones by changing lanes.

Smaller passenger vehicles use the parking lots. Buses, semis, and delivery vans stay on the road.

## Errands

Cars in the curb lanes run errands, and that's where most of the danger to pedestrians comes from: they cross the sidewalk.

1. A car picks a store, reserves a parking stall, and slows down.
2. It turns across the sidewalk into that store's driveway.
3. It parks, and the driver stays a while.
4. It backs out into the aisle, rolls to the exit, and waits for a gap.
5. It merges back into traffic.

Westbound cars serve the north side: Walgroins, Taco Hell, and the Dorf service bays. Eastbound cars serve the south side: Booger King's drive-thru, Colonial Plaza, and the Cramped Inn. Each lot has its own driveway, and cars only park where their driveway leads.

The lots stay busy. They start the day 60 to 85% full, and roughly 30 cars arrive per minute.

## Things to watch for

**Drive-thrus.** Cars queue for Booger King and Taco Hell.

**Traffic stops.** Police cruise with their lights off until they find someone. Then they light up, follow a curb-lane car, and pull it over half onto the sidewalk. Passing traffic slows, swerves around, or moves over a lane.

**Buses.** Curb-lane buses signal, stop with their door at one of four bus stops, tuck in toward the curb, and pull out once the lane is clear.

**Erratic drivers.** Some drivers wander in their lane or overtake recklessly.

**Shortcuts.** Some departing drivers skip the road: they jump the sidewalk at a few spots, or cut straight across the grass from one lot to the next. The tire ruts show where.

**Headlights.** Cars switch their headlights on at dusk.
