# Getting around

## Sprinting

Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while walking to sprint. Mats leans forward, lengthens his stride, and pumps his arms.

Sprinting drains the stamina wheel that floats next to him. A full wheel lasts about four seconds. Stop sprinting for half a second and it refills, a little faster than it drained.

If the wheel runs empty, Mats is winded: he can't sprint again until it's a quarter full. Don't empty it in the middle of the road.

## Hopping

<kbd>Space</kbd> makes Mats do a small hop.

## Shopping carts

Abandoned shopping carts sit in the parking lots.

- **Ride one:** stand next to a cart and press <kbd>Space</kbd> to climb in. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> again to climb out.
- **Push one:** stand behind a cart and press <kbd>P</kbd>. Walk to push it, and press <kbd>P</kbd> again to let go.

You can't sprint while pushing or riding a cart.

## The camera

Arrow keys move Mats relative to the camera, so ↑ always walks away from you. Turn the view with <kbd>A</kbd> and <kbd>D</kbd> (45° steps), tilt it with <kbd>W</kbd> and <kbd>S</kbd>, and press <kbd>R</kbd> to snap it back behind Mats.
